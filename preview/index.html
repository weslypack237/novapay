How money moves through NovaPay

  1. 1

    Fund your wallet

    Top up in FCFA from MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money. Your balance only changes once the provider confirms the payment.

  2. 2

    Create a virtual card

    Issue a card for one purpose — a course, a subscription, an ad account — and give it its own limit.

  3. 3

    Pay online

    Use the card at eligible international merchants. Every payment and decline appears in your history with the reason.

  4. 4

    Withdraw what's left

    Send eligible funds back to a verified Mobile Money number. Funds are held, not spent, until the payout is final.

What we won’t promise you

Payment apps lose trust by overpromising. Here is where NovaPay draws the line today.

Not every merchant will accept every card.

Acceptance depends on the issuer, the merchant's country and category, and 3-D Secure. When a payment is declined, we show you why.

We will ask who you are.

Identity checks protect your money and are required by law. We collect only what's needed and use a verification partner rather than storing your documents.

No crypto, for now.

Crypto funding and withdrawals stay switched off until there is a clear, lawful way to offer them in Cameroon.

No borrowed logos.

We'll name the card network on your card when the agreement is signed — not before.
